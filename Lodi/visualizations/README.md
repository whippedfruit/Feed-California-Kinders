Each point is one grade at one of 31 matched schools. Breakfast and lunch are reported separately per 100 estimated days of student attendance. The combined figure shows breakfasts plus lunches per estimated day of student attendance.

Estimated attendance days equal fall Census enrollment × 180 assumed school days, minus the grade’s share of absence days. California reports absences for groups of grades. The analysis allocates those days in proportion to each grade’s fall enrollment within its school and reporting group. The rates compare reported meals with estimated attendance. Recorded attendance days and counts of individual meal recipients are unavailable in these inputs.

The workbook’s exact meal-reporting dates and whether the counts are reimbursable meals remain unconfirmed. The workbook has no separate verified transitional kindergarten (TK) meal category. Preschool counts are excluded from the figures.
