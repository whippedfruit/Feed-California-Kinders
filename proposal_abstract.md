Proposal abstract (draft)

California requires public school districts, county offices of education, and charter schools serving TK–12 to make free breakfast and lunch available each school day to students who ask for a meal. Some TK and kindergarten schedules end before lunch is served. This project asks how school meal service varies across schools and whether schedule information helps explain those differences.

We will analyze California Department of Education meal claims for 2024–25 alongside available school schedule records. The claims list meals by site and month. They do not identify students’ grades or include attendance denominators, so they cannot show TK- or kindergarten-specific service rates.

Preliminary records from three Irvine Unified schools cover November 3–7, 2025. Vista Verde served about 11 kindergarten lunches per 100 kindergarten student-days attended, compared with 46 first-grade lunches per 100 first-grade student-days attended. Woodbury’s rates were 9 and 48; Bonita Canyon’s were 45 and 51. All three schools had a part-day kindergarten classification in 2024–25, though their lunch-service rates differed.

We will use the statewide and Irvine records to describe school meal service and determine which additional records would be needed to study how kindergarten schedules relate to it.
